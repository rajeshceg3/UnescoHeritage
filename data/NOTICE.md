No photographs are redistributed; separate image rights are not assumed.

Representative points are dataset coordinates, not surveyed property boundaries, entrances or travel directions. Serial/transboundary properties may span many places. Two properties have no supplied representative point. One property has no English short description. Missing values are not invented.
