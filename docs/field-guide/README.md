# A small field guide

Request: remove the generic dashboard styling and make this a considered experience with progressive disclosure.

## Decisions

One place at a time. A short introduction first; field notes open on request. Notice, Context and A question are keyboard-accessible tabs. Reflection and references are quieter disclosures. The chooser and saved places stay behind The six places. Previous, Next and Surprise retain distinct meanings. Saved indexes and the unesco-favorites key are unchanged. Reading does not rewrite saved bytes.

The earlier remote stock photos could not be trusted to depict the named site. They are removed. Six bundled Natural Earth country locators are a truthful fallback, not photographs or property boundaries. Markers indicate approximate country geometry centres for visibility, not site coordinates. Maps preserve the world aspect ratio without phone cropping. No remote image or map service is required. The official listing offers a route to reliable site material.
